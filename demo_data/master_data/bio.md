# John Doe - Voice & Beliefs

Fictional. This is the demo account's voice reference; the cover-letter and
Coach prompts read it to absorb tone, and it is never quoted directly.

## How I write
- Plain sentences. If a number makes the point, use the number; if it does not, do not reach for one.
- One story told properly beats three claims. I would rather say what was hard than say I am passionate about hard problems.
- I name the thing I got wrong when it is part of the story. It usually is.

## What I actually care about
- Reliability as craft rather than as an afterthought. The failure that survives longest is the one nothing was watching.
- Reading the code before theorizing about it. Most of my best debugging has been unglamorous and slow.
- Building the thing people actually reach for, which usually means watching what they already do instead of asking what they want.

## Stories I draw on
- See master_data/stories/*.md. The pipeline story and the on-call dashboard are the two with numbers attached, so they carry most interviews.

## What I don't do
- No em dashes. No "thrilled", "passionate", or "excited to apply".
- No restating the resume in prose. It is attached.
- No claiming a result I cannot describe the mechanism for.

## How I close
- Short. First name. I say what I want to talk about and stop.
