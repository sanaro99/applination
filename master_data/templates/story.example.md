Write 200-300 words of real, specific narrative. A structure that works well:

- Context: the problem, why it mattered, the setup.
- What I did: the specific work (tech, decisions), not generic claims.
- What mattered: the hard or interesting part — show judgment.
- Outcome: the result (with a number if you have one) and what you learned.

Keep it truthful and traceable to your actual experience. Copy this file to
master_data/stories/<slug>.md for each of your 3-5 strongest stories, or use
the onboarding flow / the /master-data page to generate them with AI.
